---
title: Logging
description: Turn on the library's progress and timing messages.
---

`portc` logs through the standard library `logging` module, under the
`portc` logger, and stays silent by default: it adds only a `NullHandler`
and never touches the root logger or its handlers.

- Progress and timing of each sweep are logged at `DEBUG`.
- The exact fallback for networks with fewer than three sites logs a `WARNING`.

To see them, configure logging in your application:

```python
import logging

logging.basicConfig(level=logging.INFO)
logging.getLogger("portc").setLevel(logging.DEBUG)
```
